# SIEVE Architecture Iseas

This document states potential architectural principles underlying SIEVE and the reasoning behind each: why the principle matters, the argument for it, the historical precedent it rests on, and why it applies to this system. It is a rationale document, not an execution plan. The organizing idea throughout is Parnas's (1972) formulation of information hiding: a system survives change when its modules hide the decisions likely to change, and its interfaces encode the decisions that must not. The seventeen principles below are the arguments in the second category — the ones whose reversal would propagate through everything.

## 1. The edge type algebra is closed and strictly typed

What flows between nodes is the single decision most capable of forcing a rewrite, because every node couples to it. SIEVE's edges carry exactly three types — dense video streams, per-frame tables aligned to a video time base, and sparse event streams — and the algebra of node signatures over these types is closed. Edges are strictly typed, with conversions performed by explicit converter nodes rather than by format negotiation, because negotiation logic is diffuse cleverness that resists review, and reviewability is the system's scarcest resource. The precedent is Bonsai (Lopes et al. 2015, *Frontiers in Neuroinformatics*), which scaled across a decade of behavioral neuroscience largely because every edge carries one uniform type — an observable sequence — so nodes compose without special cases. The deeper precedent is Codd's relational algebra: closure over a small set of types is what makes a system's expressions machine-manipulable at all.

## 2. Timestamps are the ground-truth temporal coordinate

Frame indices are node-local artifacts; timestamps are physical facts. Decimation, temporal smoothing, and warmup all change the mapping between index and wall-clock time, and any downstream table or event that records an index in some node's private coordinate system is unfixably wrong once that node changes. Every node therefore declares its time transform — rate change, offset, and temporal support region (the generalization of halo and warmup) — and all tabular and event outputs carry timestamps in canonical time. The precedent is FFmpeg's filter graph, which has survived decades of codec churn partly because presentation-timestamp propagation is a core invariant rather than a per-filter convention. The principle matters doubly for behavioral science, where inter-event intervals and durations are the measurands themselves: an architecture that cannot guarantee temporal coordinates cannot guarantee results.

## 3. The fingerprint is complete

A node's identity is the triple of operation version, parameters, and parent content hash — and this triple captures everything that affects output. No ambient configuration, no undeclared file reads, no library drift, no unseeded randomness. The argument is that caching, provenance, resumability, and testing all reduce to one theorem — same fingerprint, same output — and the theorem holds only if the fingerprint is complete. An incomplete fingerprint does not fail loudly; it rots silently, serving stale cache hits that masquerade as results. The parameter taxonomy (measurand, estimator, cosmetic) is machine-readable within this contract, which makes a strong guarantee possible: cosmetic changes provably do not invalidate scientific outputs, and live tuning can exploit that fact rather than merely hoping it.

## 4. Kernels declare their support; scheduling owns the rest

Every kernel is a pure function over a chunk plus its declared spatial and temporal support region (halo). Whether execution is lazy, streaming, tiled, or distributed across a Slurm cluster is a scheduler decision that no kernel may observe. The argument is the Dask lesson: graph definition, chunking model, and scheduler are three separate layers, and the middle one — the chunk-plus-halo contract — is the load-bearing interface that lets executors multiply without touching kernels. The alternative, kernels that quietly assume whole-clip memory residence or sequential frame arrival, converts every future execution strategy into a rewrite of every kernel. For hours-long arena recordings destined for HPC batch processing, this is not a hypothetical.

## 5. Persistent formats are versioned from their first byte

Pipeline definitions, run manifests, and the store's key scheme are user-facing persistent artifacts, and each carries a schema version with a migration path from the beginning. The argument is asymmetry: a version field costs one line now and is impossible to retrofit gracefully, because changing a hash scheme without versioning silently orphans every cached object and manifest in existence, and changing file semantics without migrations breaks every shared pipeline. A community cannot form around artifacts that a point release can invalidate. The precedent is every serialization ecosystem that survived (protobuf, SQL dialects with migration tooling) versus every one that calcified or shattered.

## 6. The graph has one canonical representation, and it is not the file format

Pipeline YAML is surface syntax. The canonical object is a normalized intermediate representation — converters inserted, defaults resolved, nodes fingerprinted — and every consumer of the graph (CLI, GUI, optimizer, future APIs) operates on the IR, never on its own reading of the text. The argument descends from the relational model and from LLVM: a closed, normalizable intermediate form is what allows machine rewriting — deduplication by content hash, dead-subtree elimination, fusion of cosmetic operations — and it is what prevents two frontends from becoming two divergent interpreters of one format. Codd's move against navigational databases was precisely this: once expressions live in a normal form, optimization becomes someone else's problem, mechanically. LLVM's typed IR turned N frontends and M backends from an N×M problem into N+M. SIEVE's GUI and CLI stay semantically identical for the same reason: they are two views of one IR.

## 7. Execution is hermetic, and re-execution is the recovery primitive

Cache validity is derived, never asserted. A step is a pure function of content-hashed inputs, so whether its cached output is current is a theorem, not a bookkeeping claim. The negative precedent is Make, whose timestamp heuristics and environmental leaks trained a generation to distrust incremental builds; the positive precedents are Bazel and Nix, which replaced dirty-flag bookkeeping with content addressing and made incrementality trustworthy at industrial scale. The same property is the fault-tolerance model: MapReduce (Dean and Ghemawat 2004) beat checkpointing and process migration because deterministic tasks make a dead worker's work simply re-executable, with no distributed state protocol to corrupt. For SIEVE on shared HPC, this is the entire failure story — jobs may die, be preempted, or run twice with no effect on correctness, and resumption is nothing more than re-walking the graph and skipping cache hits. The tax is determinism discipline: seeded randomness inside the fingerprint, pinned dependency versions inside the op version, and an honest flag on genuinely nondeterministic operations so they are never silently cached.

## 8. The core defines the node contract and never imports a node

Extension flows through a registry under dependency inversion: builtin, agent-authored, and third-party nodes all register identically, and all pass one conformance suite that tests the contract's clauses — halo honesty, time-transform correctness, fingerprint completeness. The precedent triad: ImageJ dominated microscopy for decades despite mediocre internals because its plugin boundary let thousands of labs extend it without touching the core; VST created an industry from one stable plugin contract; and scikit-learn's estimator API (Buitinck et al. 2013) supplied the crucial refinement — `check_estimator`, a conformance suite any third party runs against their own code. That refinement is the heart of the matter for SIEVE: the reviewed artifact is not the plugins but the contract plus its conformance tests. The suite is the machine-readable oracle that permits unreviewed agent-authored nodes to be trusted, which makes it the single point where human review hours buy the most leverage in the entire system.

## 9. Data carries its coordinate frame

Crop, resize, pyramid decimation, and undistortion each change the pixel coordinate system, and a detection at (x, y) in a cropped, downsampled stream is meaningless without the transform back to raw sensor coordinates. Edges therefore carry the accumulated geometric transform, and tables and events carry timestamps, raw-frame coordinates, and units as intrinsic structure rather than as convention. The argument is that conventions do not survive composition — every function boundary is an opportunity for silent axis confusion, and in a DAG the number of boundaries equals the number of edges — so the check must live in the data structure, not in documentation and discipline. The precedents run from the Mars Climate Orbiter (two correct modules, one implicit unit convention, one lost spacecraft) to the numpy-to-xarray lineage (Hoyer and Hamman 2017), in which labeled dimensions and coordinates displaced positional-axis convention for exactly this reason. This principle is the spatial twin of principle 2.

## 10. The system has a narrow, semantics-free waist

The shared middle of the system — edge types, node contract, fingerprint scheme — is deliberately minimal, and the executor that operates on it is semantics-free: it walks graphs, hashes, and schedules chunks, and never knows what a wavelet band or a halo means. Above the waist, authoring frontends multiply; below it, execution strategies multiply; the waist itself is the only thing that must never break, which is why it must be embarrassingly small. The precedent is the cleanest natural experiment in architecture: TCP/IP versus OSI. OSI standardized everything at every layer and died of it; the Internet standardized one minimal spanning layer and let unimagined technologies proliferate on both sides of it (Clark 1988; Saltzer, Reed, and Clark 1984 for the end-to-end argument). Unix pipes won the same way. Intelligence in the shared middle must serve everyone and therefore serves no one; semantics belong at the endpoints — in the nodes.

## 11. Correctness is expressed as properties and metamorphic relations

Scientific video analysis is an oracle-free domain: there is no ground truth against which to check most outputs, and example-based tests generated by running the code merely encode the implementation — Weyuker's non-testable-programs problem (1982), and the documented failure of SIEVE's own v3 suite. The resolution is twofold. Property-based testing (Claessen and Hughes 2000) specifies implementation-free invariants and shrinks failures to minimal counterexamples. Metamorphic testing (surveyed in Segura et al. 2016) checks relations between outputs when no single output can be checked: cropping then detecting agrees with detecting then transforming; decimation changes timestamps exactly as declared and nothing else; cosmetic perturbation leaves measurands within declared tolerance. These relations are restatements of the contracts, which is precisely why they survive rewrites that examples do not — and why they must be authored by the human who owns the contracts, never by the agent that wrote the code under test. Golden masters persist only in a small, curated role: catching unintended change, with human blessing as an explicit step.

## 12. Ingest parses; it does not validate

Container and codec reality is hostile — presentation timestamps that start anywhere, variable-frame-rate files claiming constant rate, B-frame reordering, decoders whose seek lands on a preceding keyframe and silently returns the wrong frame. The principle, from the language-security literature's critique of Postel's law, is that every point which interprets input is a parser, and inconsistent parsers are where bugs live; the winning discipline is to parse once, at one reviewed boundary, into a canonical internal representation that is correct by construction. SIEVE's ingest assigns every frame a canonical timestamp from container PTS, verifies claimed timing by full scan on first contact, builds a content-addressed frame index, and implements exact-frame seeking as index lookup rather than codec seek. Decoder identity and version live in the ingest fingerprint, because decoder builds are not bit-stable across platforms. The stakes are distinctive here: getting ingest wrong does not force a rewrite of code so much as a rewrite of results — a silent off-by-three frame error survives every downstream contract and invalidates analyses years later. Everything principle 2 promises stands on this foundation.

## 13. The store separates immutable objects from mutable references

Materialized outputs, frame indexes, and manifests are content-addressed immutable objects; runs and pinned results are a thin mutable layer of named references into the object graph; liveness is reachability from references, and eviction below that line is pure policy. The precedent is git's object model — and Nix's store, which repeats it — where immutability made concurrency, integrity checking, and distribution trivial because there is no update-in-place failure mode, and where garbage collection is a derived operation rather than a bookkeeping chore. The alternative, a directory tree of outputs governed by timestamps and naming conventions, is the Make failure mode transposed to storage: at the scale of hours of video across replicates and pyramid levels, it ends in either disk exhaustion or a delete-everything culture that defeats caching. Key scheme and object granularity are baked into every stored byte, which is why they are decided with the architecture rather than discovered later.

## 14. Experiments generate graphs; they do not extend the executor

Parameter sweeps, arena replicates, and cross-run aggregation are graph generation, not graph execution: a layer above the waist takes a pipeline template, a parameter grid, and a clip manifest, emits many SIEVE runs, and consumes their outputs as a dataset. SIEVE's side of that boundary is only two things — machine-readable run manifests, and template instantiation as a first-class IR operation, so that fifty estimator variants share their cached preprocessing prefix and recompute only the suffix. The precedent is the workflow-manager outcome in bioinformatics: Snakemake and Nextflow (Köster and Rahmann 2012) won not by absorbing tools but by orchestrating black boxes that communicate through files, because the file boundary keeps every tool swappable and debuggable in isolation. The negative precedent is visible in every aging lab codebase: the analysis framework that grew a loop over videos, then a configuration matrix, then plotting, until the core could no longer change without breaking someone's figure script. Keeping the experiment layer outside the waist is what prevents SIEVE from becoming that codebase.

## 15. Effects are declared before they are enforced

Every node declares its effects — pure by default, or external reads, network, GPU, nondeterminism — with declared reads entering the fingerprint, whether or not anything yet restricts them. The argument is an ecosystem-dynamics one: retrofitting a permission model onto extensions that assume ambient authority breaks everything at once, which is how Mozilla's XUL add-on ecosystem died in a single traumatic migration, while Chrome's declared-permission manifests allowed enforcement to tighten incrementally around declarations that already existed. The intellectual lineage runs through capability security and Haskell's IO type: making effects visible in the signature is valuable independent of restriction. For a system intended to distribute community-authored and agent-authored nodes, the declaration is the hook that makes future sandboxing an addition rather than a rupture.

## 16. Numerical equality is defined in the contract, not invented per test

Bit-exact reproducibility across hardware is unachievable — GPU reduction order, fused multiply-add, and math-library differences are real — and the reproducible-neuroimaging literature (Glatard et al. 2015) demonstrated that identical pipelines yield measurably different scientific outputs across platforms from floating-point library variation alone. The resolution is neither demanding bit-exactness everywhere nor tolerating silence: edges carry a small dtype and colorspace lattice with promotion rules decided once, and every measurand declares its comparison semantics — bit-exact, absolute epsilon, relative epsilon, or distributional. Conformance tests, golden masters, and replay validation read tolerance from the contract rather than inventing epsilons locally, because locally invented epsilons re-encode whatever the implementation happened to do, which is the v3 test-suite disease in numerical form.

## 17. The compatibility promise is explicit, and stability is a feature with a release date

A stated compatibility policy is social architecture: it is what makes third-party investment rational. The natural experiment is Linux's "we do not break userspace" — internal APIs churn freely while the syscall boundary is sacred, and a quarter-century of ecosystem confidence followed — against Python 3's decade of migration purgatory. Rust's edition system supplies the synthesis: breakage is permitted, but opt-in per artifact, with old editions supported and automated migration shipping alongside the break. The two failure modes this principle guards against are symmetric: an eternal freeze ossifies the system and forbids fixing discovered flaws in the core, while unmanaged breakage kills the community investment the project exists to attract. Accordingly, the pre-1.0 posture is an explicit declaration that everything may break, and the 1.0 promise covers exactly the waist — pipeline schema, node contract, store format, manifest schema — with versioned migration as the price of any break. A promise made after the first painful break is worthless; made before anyone depends on the system, it is the reason they can.
