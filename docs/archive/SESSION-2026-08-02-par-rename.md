This overturns the naming settled earlier the same day in
SESSION-2026-08-02-record-class.md, where `AR-0001` ("Architecture
Rationale") was flagged as technically the most correct candidate but
rejected in favor of `ARCH-0001`. PAR is that rejected candidate
returning with a P and a mnemonic that carries the system's purpose —
worth keeping visible for re-examination, since the position that lost
here had won hours earlier.

Execution notes, per living-record doctrine rather than re-argued:
frozen archive records keep their `ARCH-NNNN` citations untouched;
numbers are unchanged, so the mapping is 1:1 and is stated once in
PAR-0001's "Form and name." "Minimal PADding" lands as the recorded
nickname of the anti-bureaucracy invariant, not a rename of it: PAR-0002
is a distillation and reports its source's vocabulary.

## Exchange 4 — Challenges become the tradeoff log; friction is stated, never inferred

The agent's answer to Exchange 3's question, compressed: not free as-is.
"Doubts raised" implies an arguer, and the adjacent exclusion of
confirming evidence invites the symmetric reading that un-argued
evidence is excluded wholesale; since a Challenges section is read as a
license for what may be entered, the ambiguity resolves badly in both
directions — unlogged friction or scrapbook creep. The asymmetry
offered: friction is incident-shaped (natural trigger, a doubt entry's
terminal form), vindication is scrapbook-shaped (no trigger, unbounded).
Proposed: a one-clause amendment admitting "friction observed against it
that did not rise to changing it."

Kendrick's ruling, verbatim and in full (a first draft, interrupted,
said: "go ahead and include all my messages for this session verbatim,
not summarized. Push and let me know when I can clear."):

> "lets go ahead and include the back and forth here verbatim more or
> less. Friction with the architecture needs to be deliberately stated,
> not inferred. All of the challenge sections have been added this way.
> Agents can point it out, but humans must confirm. And fyi, the
> friction is breadcrumbs for the human more than the agent. It doesn't
> creep towards a scrapbook, it creeps towards a log. Agents can't
> determine the tipping point for when some architectural rationale
> *needs* to be improved, because by the nature of calling it
> architecture, it's followed by the agent by default. You can add this
> to challenge for the challenge part of the PAR system specifically,
> and add the rest as improving the rationale and outcomes itself.
> Challenge serves as a pros/cons log which can be assessed as breaking
> (prompting architecture adjustments) or not breaking. It is quite
> literally what you said: the resolution of doubt or the lack of
> resolution of doubt. Every architectural decision has tradeoffs,
> architectural decisions need ways of pointing to what those tradeoffs
> are without having to change in response to them. It produced an
> incident in that theres no way to literally log doubts that matter,
> which is breaking, resulting in the PAR system needing to be tuned,
> but because adjusting the challenge system as a proxy for a tradeoff
> log, literally pros and cons, doesn't land with anything else, by
> 0001's own rule it can land in 0001 as improving, which the PAR system
> itself has a mechanism to accomodate. So I agree, friction as unargued
> evidence of it being friction is not useful, so it should land with
> the reason it is friction (doubt that does exist but isn't changing),
> or can be dismissed (the PAR entry withstanding the decision)."

Executed against PAR-0001, per the ruling's own split. The wording gap
was breaking — doubts that matter but change nothing had no place to
land — so the Challenges paragraphs are rewritten as the tradeoff log:
entries carry the resolution of doubt or the lack of it, friction is
stated and human-confirmed rather than inferred, each entry is assessed
breaking or not breaking, and a pro enters only paired with the con it
answers. A consequence is added naming the human as the reader who calls
the tipping point. The scrapbook-creep doubt, raised by the agent and
dismissed, lands as the section's first entry about itself. Also per the
form ruling above: Exchanges 2 and 3 upgraded to full verbatim messages.
