# Session: records state outcomes

Status: Frozen
Date: 2026-08-02

The argument that added *outcomes* to the record form (PAR-0001). It
arose inside the debt-advancement sitting
(`SESSION-2026-08-02-debt-advance.md`) and is separable from it:
nothing here depends on the debt content.

## Exchange 1 — does the form already require this?

Kendrick, having just articulated the debt system's intended end state:
"the other important outcome here is that the debt PAR becomes the true
planning surface. does PAR0001 define PARs as listing the explicit
goals of the PAR? if not, that should probably surface quite high and
be very human-readable, and generally prioritized. I think it basically
does, yeah?"

The check came back no. The required form was "a status line, a date,
context, the decision, and consequences" — no goals, no required
placement, no prominence. What made "basically does" plausible: a
Context section opens with the problem, and a problem statement
half-carries the aim (PAR-0002's "answer, continuously and truthfully,
what it owes"). What killed the near-miss: a problem is where the
decision started, not where it steers. The end state Kendrick had just
articulated — the automatic ledger de facto, `DEBT.md` rare-to-empty,
planning as orderings over the ledger — existed nowhere but the
conversation.

## Exchange 2 — the name, and the occasion as evidence

Kendrick: "maybe stated as outcomes or something. also, you managed to
drop the numbering idea as well as the two explicit important stated
outcomes of the debt PAR when working as intended."

Two things landed at once. The name: *outcomes* — the correction was
accepted without further argument, "goals" dying as the label for the
same content. And the correction was itself the argument's best
evidence: within one sitting, the stated outcomes had been dropped from
working state by the agent. End states that live only in conversation
do not survive the conversation, which is the precise failure mode
surfacing them in the record removes.

## Decision

PAR-0001's required form gains outcomes: an `## Outcomes` section
placed directly after the status and date, before Context — the first
thing a reader meets — stating in human-readable prose what the system
looks like working as intended. Distinct from Consequences by
direction: Consequences report what follows from the decision as made;
outcomes state the end state it steers toward. Phrased as intention,
not current fact. Existing records gain theirs at the next substantive
edit — no retro-stamping sweep. PAR-0001 self-applies in the amendment
this primary underpins; PAR-0002's lands with the debt-advance
amendments; the Proposed drafts (PAR-0003, PAR-0004) gain theirs at
their design sessions.

## What lost

- The status quo — Context's problem statement suffices. Lost to the
  direction distinction in Exchange 1.
- "Goals" as the name. Lost by Kendrick's call ("maybe stated as
  outcomes or something"); outcomes read as an end state checkable
  against the tree where goals read as aspiration, and the point was
  not argued further.
