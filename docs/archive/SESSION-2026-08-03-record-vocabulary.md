# Session — the word "record" retired from working surfaces (2026-08-03)

Status: Frozen

One small decision, argued immediately after PAR-0006's acceptance and
frozen at its close: what a rationale is called, and where the word
*record* may still appear.

## Exchange 1 — the correction, and the contradiction named

Kendrick, on the agent's prose and the how-to bullets it had just
committed: "These aren't records. You're misusing that again. This is
not ADR, and I don't use ADRs."

The agent owned the slip — calling PAR-0006 "the record" all session,
and writing "a record that cannot be told from the user's chair" into
the acceptance how-to — and named the contradiction rather than fixing
silently: the tier-1 texts carry the same usage. `AGENTS.md` said
"*Record* is the genus: a rationale, a primary, a stub"; PAR-0001 says
"Records are living." The diagnosis both sides accepted: *record* is
ADR vocabulary, and it re-imports exactly the baggage the ARCH→PAR
rename retired — immutable, archival, one per decision — every time an
agent reads it. A rationale is a living argument; the only honest
records in the repo are the frozen primaries. The agent recommended a
repo-wide sweep, the word being the vector.

## Exchange 2 — the ruling: targeted, not a sweep

Kendrick: "Yeah lets get rid of record because you keep pattern
matching to it, at least dropped from the problematic places. We don't
have to do a whole sweep basically. Every architecture rationale change
gets it's session saved is fine more or less."

Ruling: drop *record*-for-rationale from the working surfaces agents
load — `AGENTS.md` (the genus line becomes "three kinds of writing:
rationale, primary, stub"; "Where records go" becomes "Where things
go") and the acceptance how-to — plus one governing sentence in
PAR-0001's not-ADR paragraph. *Record* keeps its honest meaning for
frozen primaries. PAR-0001's own older prose cleans as the file is
next rewritten, deliberately not in a sweep. Position that lost: the
full sweep (the agent's recommendation), declined as unnecessary. The
primary rule is reaffirmed in plain words in passing: every
architecture rationale change gets its session saved.
