decisions:
  - what: three modules, split by what each can be tested without
    why: plan needs no codec; three callers want the static half without a VideoReader
  - what: lead-in is a backward max over Dag.order, never a path enumeration
    why: input_warmup_frames is monotone, so node-by-node max equals max-over-paths
  - what: input_warmup_frames extracted from source_warmup_frames
    why: the walk needs the step without the path; two copies of the conversion is what that docstring forbids
  - what: cache keyed by (node key, source frame index)
    why: a key identifies a computation, not a span — partial coverage becomes ordinary rather than corrupt
  - what: kernels resolved with a one-element preference, pinned to plan.backend
    why: a GPU fallback would store GPU output under a cpu-derived key
  - what: WINDOWED, rate_changing, and multi-upstream nodes refused at run time, not plan time
    why: the graph is valid and its plan is useful; only the call is impossible
  - what: params validated for every node, not only cacheable ones
    why: node_keys validates as a side effect of hashing and skips what it cannot hash
  - what: decode_start clamps at 0 and reports lead_in_shortfall
    why: no footage would fix a clip at frame 3 behind 30 frames of warmup

rejected:
  - what: cache-aware lead-in shortening
    why: only sound if an entry covered the lead-in span, which the store does not record; slow-and-correct wins by cache_key.py's asymmetry rule
  - what: static pruning of nodes whose output is fully cached
    why: per-frame lookup plus lazy decode already gives the win without a coverage model
  - what: a bounded MemoryFrameStore
    why: no measurement of what a tuning session holds exists; a bound picked now would be picked from nothing
