decisions:
  - what: scrub_to_repaint moved 50 ms -> 100 ms
    why: 46.7 ms of the 67.8 ms round trip is the container seek and it has no knob
  - what: the new ceiling is enforced by degrading, not by asserting
    why: a budget nothing acts on is a wish; ScrubPolicy snaps to a 1 s grid when the median of the last 5 scrubs exceeds it
  - what: new scrub_settle budget at 250 ms
    why: the release must always land exactly, and its worst case is two decodes — a different quantity from the drag and it needed its own number
  - what: requests carry intent (EXACT / SCRUB / PLAYBACK)
    why: only guesses may be snapped or dropped, and only scrub round trips may count toward degradation
  - what: the notice says "coarse seek", not "keyframe seek"
    why: the measurement says keyframe alignment is not the mechanism, so naming it that would encode a false model in the UI

rejected:
  - what: hardware-accelerated decode
    why: does not engage in this OpenCV build — measured, not assumed
  - what: keyframe-aligned seeking
    why: no sawtooth in seek cost across 150 consecutive targets, so it buys nothing
  - what: skipping the YUV to BGR conversion
    why: saves ~6 ms and returns a buffer that cannot be displayed
