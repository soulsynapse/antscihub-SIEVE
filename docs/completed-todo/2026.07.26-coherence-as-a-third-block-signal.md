decisions:
  - what: the emitted scalar is ((λ2-λ3)/(λ2+λ3))², not the spec's ((λ1-λ2)/(λ1+λ2))²
    why: the drafted formula fails the item's own translation test (0.16, not ~1);
      the spec named the test as the arbiter, so the formula was treated as the
      draft — see the linked finding
  - what: the tensor is block-reduced before the eigendecomposition
    why: a per-pixel tensor is near rank-one and votes "coherent" everywhere;
      only block aggregation of mixed orientations makes the spectrum mean
      anything — the mirror of the LK solve-before-reduction constraint
  - what: exactly-zero temporal change is gated to 0
    why: a changeless block's null direction is the t-axis itself and the raw
      scalar would read a vacuous 1; near-zero change still reads high, which
      is why the doc says coherence conditions change_energy and never
      detects alone
  - what: one dict entry in SIGNAL_LABELS is the entire GUI change
    why: the quick-switch builds its buttons and the per-signal value-band
      memory from that mapping, so the third signal inherited both

rejected:
  - what: a change_energy-weighted or thresholded-energy variant of the scalar
    why: magnitude is the other channel's job; folding it in would re-couple
      the two things the two-channel rule exists to keep orthogonal, and any
      energy threshold would reintroduce the lighting-dependence coherence
      exists to escape

measurements:
  source: synthetic 96x96 blurred-noise fields, the suite's textured()
  results:
    - uniform 1 px/frame translation: 0.924 median interior coherence
    - strong in-place flicker: 0.057
    - "opposing ±2 px halves in one 96 px block: 0.010 — 0.496 if the eigendecomposition is swapped before the reduction"
