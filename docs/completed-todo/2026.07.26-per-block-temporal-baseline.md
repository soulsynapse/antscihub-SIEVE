settled:
  - what: The warmup split on the filter contract
    where: "`src/sieve/core/filter_base.py`"
    do_not_redecide: >
      `FilterSpec.warmup_frames` is the *bound* over the legal parameter
      range; `ParamsBase.warmup_frames` is the *configured* need.
      `node_warmup_frames` picks. Declaring only the bound charges every run
      for the worst case (7199 frames, in the case that forced the split).
  - what: Warmup along a path
    where: "`src/sieve/core/filter_base.py` `source_warmup_frames`"
    do_not_redecide: >
      It does **not** sum. It walks sink to root converting `need` to
      `ceil(need / output_rate)`. A plain sum compiles, runs, and under-
      warms every temporal filter behind a decimator while rendering a
      plausible frame.
  - what: Threshold units
    where: "`src/sieve/filters/temporal_baseline.py`"
    do_not_redecide: >
      Median and MAD over a trailing window, because the events are in the
      sample. The estimate lags a step change by about one window; a centred
      one needs `Mode.WINDOWED`, which is refused.

summary: >
  A threshold can now be denominated in a block's own deviations rather than in
  the lighting rig's intensity units, and a filter whose lead-in is a parameter
  can charge for the lead-in it actually needs instead of the worst case over
  its whole legal range.

decisions:
  - what: median and MAD, not mean and standard deviation
    why: the events are in the sample and would inflate the spread they are measured against
  - what: warmup became params-derived — FilterSpec's field is now a bound, ParamsBase's method the need
    why: a static declaration for this filter is 7199 frames against a typical need of 149
  - what: the refinement may only shrink the bound, and node_warmup_frames refuses otherwise
    why: under-warming is silent and over-warming is merely slow — the same asymmetry cache_key.py runs on
  - what: one node computing both statistics, not a baseline node feeding a standardize node over two ports
    why: emits is still one stream per node, so handing over median *and* MAD needs two rings and two identical medians
  - what: the window is sampled at a stride, at most 256 frames held
    why: a median's standard error falls as 1/sqrt(n), and a 7200-sample partition per frame is unaffordable
  - what: a cell with zero MAD borrows the frame's median nonzero spread
    why: zero blinds the detector on the quietest cells and infinity fires on anything; a constant floor breaks affine invariance
  - what: window_seconds in seconds with an fps parameter, not window_frames
    why: both failure modes it trades off — a bout outlasting the window, drift outlasting the estimate — are stated in time

rejected:
  - what: a centred window
    why: needs future frames, which is Mode.WINDOWED, which the executor refuses
  - what: emitting the spread as a third mode
    why: deviation is already denominated in it; baseline is the mode that diagnoses a wrong window
  - what: accepting integer dtypes
    why: this holds up to 256 copies of its input — trivial on a block grid, 1.5 GB on a 1080p frame
