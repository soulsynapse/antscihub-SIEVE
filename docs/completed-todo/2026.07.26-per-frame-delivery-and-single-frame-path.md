decisions:
  - what: the consumer rides RenderRequest and runs on the render thread, after the staleness check
    why: six hundred queued signals per render would spend the GUI thread on marshalling; the check ordering is what makes a superseded render contribute nothing
  - what: request_frame reuses the whole submission machinery (revision, latest-wins, one pending slot)
    why: a hover that outruns its renders must coalesce exactly like band drags; a second path would need its own staleness rules
  - what: request_frame does not arm filter_to_first_tick
    why: the budget is the tab's first graph tick; a wizard hover is not that interval
  - what: SeriesCollector refuses non-contiguous indices
    why: a gap is frames lost in delivery, and a series with a silent hole is a detector input that lies about when nothing happened
