# The graph HUD

The last piece of what `2026.07.26-the-first-live-graph-tick.md` split: the
producer existed and was tested, and nothing drew it. `GraphHud` is that view,
in the filter tab's left column under the D row — x is the source frame index
across the working window, y is milliseconds for that frame, and the playhead
is `VideoPlayer.frame_changed` in one more view, exactly as it is for every
other plot in the `BandPlot` family.

## What suppressing the handles buys

The base class's one-gesture contract is that a drag near a handle moves the
handle and any other drag scrubs. The HUD has no threshold to place — so
`_grabbable` refuses every press and the whole surface scrubs. That is not
merely the absence of a feature: a cost spike on this plot is a *place* in the
clip, and the gesture to go and look at it is the same drag the user makes on
the scalogram. Without the override, the base parks dimmed arming handles at
the range edges, and a drag along the top of the plot would have silently
created a band no code reads.

## What was checked by mutation

Two deliberate breakages, each failing exactly the test written against it:

- Replacing `_schedule_repaint()` with a direct `update()` in `add_cost` —
  the repaint-per-point failure the TODO item warned about — fails
  `test_a_burst_of_points_coalesces_to_one_trailing_repaint` at its first
  assertion: the burst produced repaints before the trailing flush.
- Deleting `self._costs.clear()` from `begin` — appending across renders —
  fails `test_begin_replaces_the_series_instead_of_appending` with both old
  points still present.

## What changed outside the item's scope

Nothing structural. `MainWindow._on_render_finished`'s status-bar summary,
which the first-tick entry called a placeholder for the HUD, is deliberately
left in place: it carries the store's reuse share, which the HUD does not
paint, and the two say different things about the same render.
