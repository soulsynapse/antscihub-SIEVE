decisions:
  - what: GraphHud subclasses BandPlot with _grabbable and _paint_handles suppressed
    why: the frame, span mapping, playhead, and scrub gesture are the conventions the item said to write over; two overrides are smaller than restating them
  - what: the series is a dict keyed by source frame index
    why: arrival order is not the axis, and a re-delivered index replaces its point rather than growing the series
  - what: the repaint throttle is a trailing single-shot armed in add_cost
    why: each frame_cost is its own queued event, so Qt's paint compression cannot coalesce across them; one flush trails the burst and no point is dropped
  - what: the y ceiling autoscales with a 10 ms floor
    why: a fast render should read as a low flat line, not as full-scale noise over a sub-millisecond ceiling
  - what: whole-render samples are drawn on the HUD via show_sample, connected in MainWindow
    why: the adapter is the window's; the tab keeps not knowing the bus has a Qt side
  - what: the span is re-read from the document on render_started, not taken from the request
    why: a wizard hover's single-frame render is one dot at its place in the working window, not a window of its own
  - what: no per-frame budget line is painted
    why: the per-frame interval deliberately has no key in BUDGETS — six hundred samples of one interval with no ceiling of its own

rejected:
  - what: keeping the series through single-frame renders (wizard hovers)
    why: the runner's promise is per-revision, and a HUD that second-guessed which renders count would own staleness the runner already owns; the wizard covers the tab while hovering anyway
  - what: using BandPlot's set_readout for the budget verdicts
    why: the base paints the readout in one fixed pen, and a miss that is not red is a defect reported in camouflage
