# The metric bus

## What was checked by mutation

Moving the clock read in `MetricBus.measure` to *after* the subscriber dispatch
loop — the real form of the bug this module is shaped around — failed
`test_a_subscribers_own_cost_stays_out_of_the_sample` and
`test_the_bus_judges_the_miss_rather_than_the_caller`. That is the ordering the
whole design point rests on, and it is invisible in the code: the two-line body
looks the same either way, and a real clock could not tell them apart without
asserting on wall-clock numbers.

## What changed outside the item's scope

`VideoPlayer.__init__` grew a `metrics` argument alongside `policy`, injectable
for the same reason and one more: `METRICS` is process-wide, so a test asserting
on what was published would otherwise hear every other test's player.

`_on_frame_ready` now reads `round_trip_ms()` once into a local and hands it to
both consumers. It was previously read inline as the argument to
`ScrubPolicy.observe`; two calls would have been two different numbers, because
the clock moves between them.

## The one thing this deliberately does not do

`cut_to_ready` and the four in-pipeline budgets are still checked by nothing.
That is not an oversight of this item — it is what the item was for. Three of
those four belong to `pipeline/preview.py` and `gui/graph_hud.py`, neither of
which exists, and instrumenting them afterwards is the retrofit this landed
first to avoid. What changed is that the retrofit is no longer possible to need:
a module that wants to report a span now subscribes to something rather than
inventing a callback.

`scrub_to_repaint` is the producer that exists today, and it was chosen because
the number was already being computed. It is not a new measurement — it is the
first one that anything other than `ScrubPolicy`'s private decision can see.
