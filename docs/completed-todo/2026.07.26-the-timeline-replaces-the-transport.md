# The timeline replaces the transport

## What the seeker cost by living in a tab

`ClipSlider` and `ClipStrip` were correct and are gone. Their arithmetic was
Qt's — `sliderPositionFromValue` over the groove less the handle width — which
is exactly right for a control with a handle and buys nothing for a band that
has none. The band spans the whole asset edge to edge, so the mapping is
`frame / frame_count * width` and the awkward part moves elsewhere: a frame owns
a *column*, not a boundary, which is why `span()` takes `end` rather than
`end - 1` and why the playhead is drawn at `centre_of_frame`. Getting that wrong
paints a one-frame window zero pixels wide.

## The rule that is not a rendering detail

`mark_clip_in` past the out point used to release the out point to the end of
the source, so the span's *length* changed under a gesture that was about its
*position*. Nothing was wrong with the code; the model could not express what
the user meant. Origin-plus-length can, and the clamp is where it shows: a
window pushed off the end of the source **moves** back to rest against the end
at full length rather than shrinking. Shrinking is the failure mode nobody
notices, because the only way to see it is to read the length back.

`ended_at` keeps the mirror rule from the old model — an end at or before the
origin releases the origin — because that one was right: the user who marks out
before their in point has decisively left the old span.

## Why `window` and `clip` are two properties

The item's point 5 says a session always has a window and warns that this must
not become a document rule. Those pull opposite ways, and the resolution is that
only one of them is *stored*. `ReplicateDocument.clip` is the user's choice and
stays `None` until they make one; `ReplicateDocument.window` is
`effective_window(clip, frames, fps)` and is never `None` while a source is
bound. Opening a video and saving immediately still writes `clip:` absent, which
is what `pipeline/plan.py` turns into the whole video and what the HPC handoff
produces by dropping the field. Clearing the clip returns the *view* to the
default ten seconds rather than to no window at all, which is the one place a
reader might expect "clear" to leave an empty band and will not find one.

`bind_source` grew an `fps` parameter for this and only this: ten seconds is a
count of frames, and nothing else in the document is in the time domain.

## What was checked by mutation

Three deliberate breakages, each caught by the test written for it and by
nothing else:

- `moved_to` clamped by shortening instead of moving — failed
  `test_a_window_pushed_off_the_end_rests_against_it_at_full_length` (all three
  parametrisations) and its GUI-level twin, and nothing in the strip or paint
  tests.
- `playback_step` wrapping without emitting `stop - 1` first — failed only
  `test_the_last_frame_is_shown_before_the_wrap`. Worth noting that no other
  playback test moved: an overshooting clock is the *ordinary* case on this
  footage, and a suite that only tested a clock landing inside the window would
  have passed the broken version.
- `Geometry.frame_at` dividing by `width - 1`, which is what V1 does — failed the
  round trip at frames 499 and 998 and passed at 0, 1, and 999. That pattern is
  the argument for parametrising the ends *and* the middle: two of the five
  cases would have hidden it.

## What changed outside the item's scope

- `ReplicateDocument` gained a `source_changed` signal. `structure_changed` also
  fires per added replicate, and the timeline's whole horizontal axis is the
  source's length — rebuilding it on a row insert would reset the window spin
  boxes under a user typing into them.
- The timeline listens to `source_changed` rather than to `VideoPlayer.opened`,
  though the player learns of a new video first. `MainWindow` binds the document
  from `opened`, so a bar on the same signal would read whichever length Qt's
  slot ordering happened to hand it.
- "Go to Start" now means the start of the *window*. Nothing was done to the
  action; `seek` clamps, and there is no other meaning left for it to have.
- `format_timecode` moved from `replicate_tab.py` to `timeline_bar.py` with the
  readout it serves. The tab has no timecode any more.
- `_fit_clip` on the document is now a call to `timeline_model.fitted`, so the
  "a saved span that lands past the end is no span" rule has one implementation
  rather than two.
- `tests/gui/conftest.py`'s document fixture binds at 30 fps. It bound no frame
  rate before, which under the new fallback would have made every test's default
  window the whole 1000-frame source and quietly hidden the ten-second rule.
- `test_the_transport_stays_with_the_player` in `test_window_state.py` asserted
  the thing this item supersedes. It is replaced by two: the tab holds no
  transport at all, and the strip is below the tab widget and not inside it.

## What is deliberately not here

The coverage and detection lanes are in `docs/LATER.md` and stay there —
nothing in this repo records what was examined or what was found, so painting
them means inventing a coverage model against zero data. `STRIP_HEIGHT` is 44
rather than the 10 the old strip used because those lanes land in this band,
and a band that grows when they arrive is a band whose geometry gets re-derived
then.

Continuous window dragging — the window following the pointer through a drag
rather than moving on press and release — needs `QUndoCommand.mergeWith` so one
gesture is one undo step. That is the same machinery the open "Drag existing
boxes" item needs, and it should be written once, there.
