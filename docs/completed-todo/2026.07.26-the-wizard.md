decisions:
  - what: the wizard proposes whole LiveChain values; the tab stays the one render owner
    why: adopt/rebuild/submit/derive already exists for every chain edit — a wizard that rendered for itself would race the tab on one latest-wins stream and copy state the mockups proved must not be copied
  - what: every provisional derives from the snapshot, never from the previous proposal
    why: switching candidates must compare alternatives, not compound them; it also makes Cancel a single value restore
  - what: hover = request_frame (video only), selection and settings edits = window render
    why: learning 4's two tiers against real costs — a candidate sweep must not queue window renders, and the selection's graphs are judged against the full working window
  - what: the wizard's video frame is grabbed out of the window render the tab was submitting anyway
    why: the playhead frame is one of the frames the graphs already paid for; a second render per selection would double the expensive tier
  - what: enabled means the hypothetical full chain grades conflict-free; duplicates block first
    why: one rule subsumes fits-the-seam and breaks-below, and learning 8's default (block duplicates until a real chain needs repetition) is a per-entry `repeatable` flag left false everywhere
  - what: catalog entries carry kinds and stage; the registry supplies only summary, version, and params defaults
    why: the cannot-type-vision finding — ArraySpec cannot say what travels between steps, so kinds stay a chain-model concept exactly as chain_model.py documents
  - what: non-parity steps get card bodies generated from their params model (gui/param_form.py)
    why: the § 2 rule that every step's parameters live in its card must hold for anything the wizard can insert, and per-filter GUI code would break non-negotiable #3 at the widget layer
  - what: § 8 settled — value bands are remembered per signal on the quick-switch
    why: the mockup's `--shot lk` consequence — a Jtt-tuned threshold silently reinterpreted in LK px/s; frequency (Hz) and count threshold (fraction of blocks) are unit-stable and carry over
  - what: § 8 settled — the wizard hosts video + density + count, no scalogram
    why: a candidate is judged by what it does to the green; the frequency band is tuned on the tab's scalogram, which stays visible behind the inset when the temporal card is reachable

rejected:
  - what: reusing v1-mockup popover/catalogue two-stage picker
    why: the tab mockup superseded it — the inset helper is the configuration surface, and the plan consolidated on it
  - what: borrowing the tab's density/count plots into the wizard (as the mockup did)
    why: learning 6 — PySide parent-death on reparented widgets; the wizard gets its own instances bound to shared state through the same tab handlers
  - what: request_frame after the selection's window render for the video pane
    why: one latest-wins stream — the second submission would abandon the first mid-window and the graphs would never update
