decisions:
  - what: >
      Gray frames leave `DecodeWorker` as `QImage.Format_Grayscale8`, not as
      the GRAY2BGR convert the item text assumed.
    why: >
      The import contract forbids `cv2` in `gui/` (opencv-containment), and Qt
      renders Grayscale8 natively — same untouched `video_view.py`, with the
      0.56 ms convert deleted rather than paid. The item's stated reason for
      BGR888 ("so the view and overlays are untouched") holds either way,
      because overlays are painted by QPainter over the image, not into it.
  - what: >
      A format flip bumps the coalescer's generation, exactly as a source
      change does.
    why: >
      The decode in flight answers the old format and cannot be recalled; the
      stamp is what stops one colour frame being painted or cached into a
      gray viewport. Same hazard as a source change, same mechanism, and the
      test drives both in one event-loop turn to force the ordering.
  - what: >
      "A render is filling" is `PreviewRunner.window_render_changed`, derived
      from the two request slots with single-frame renders excluded.
    why: >
      Composite refreshes and wizard hovers go through the same runner once
      per playhead move; counting them would flap the viewport's format at
      playback rate. Deriving the flag from the slots rather than keeping a
      second state machine means every path that starts, supersedes,
      finishes, fails, abandons, or closes a render updates it for free.
  - what: >
      The effective format is `manual or (rendering and not pinned)`, owned
      by `GrayToggle`; the player only obeys `set_viewport_luma`, which is
      deliberately not part of `apply_preferences`.
    why: >
      The effective format is not the stored preference — auto-gray and the
      per-render colour pin both live outside the store — so routing it
      through preferences would make the store lie about what is on screen.
      One owner for the policy keeps the player a transport.
  - what: >
      Clicking an auto-engaged or manually-gray toggle during a render always
      yields colour — clearing manual state and pinning in one gesture.
    why: >
      A manual un-toggle that silently fell back to auto-gray would be a
      click that visibly did nothing, which is the worst reading a control
      can have. The pin dies when the render episode ends, not with each
      resubmission, so a knob burst is one episode.

rejected:
  - what: A preferences-dialog checkbox as the control surface.
    why: >
      The item's own point — the person who needs it is watching a stuttering
      pane right now and does not know colour is what they are paying for.
      The preference persists, but the affordance is on the viewport.

# A grayscale viewport the user can reach from the viewport

The falsifier from the item stands and is worth restating: if the pane
changing appearance unbidden reads as a fault in practice, retreat to the
purely manual toggle — `GrayToggle.set_rendering` is the one connection to
unplug, and the manual half is unchanged by construction.

## The item as written (trimmed)

Priority **1 of 2**, ahead of docs/todo/render-fed-playback.md: the pipeline
decodes luma as of 2026.07.27 but the viewport still read full-resolution BGR
at 22.7 ms a frame against the luma path's 9.9. From
`docs/findings/2026.07.27-decode-is-a-bandwidth-wall-shared-by-two-consumers.md`:

```
                              alone            during a render
player colour            23.2 ms  43.1 fps    50.9 ms  19.6 fps
player luma               9.9 ms 100.8 fps    19.1 ms  52.3 fps
```

Gray is 2.7x on the number the user experiences during a render, and the only
configuration above real time at all. Decided 2026-07-27: the control lives on
the viewport and says what it buys; off by default; reading (b) — the pane
drops to gray on its own while a render fills, announced by the same control
that overrides it, and a click during the render pins colour for that render.
Reopening the capture is the flip's mechanism (format is fixed at capture
construction); the playhead survives it; `ProxyFrameCache` is dropped because
it is keyed by index and says nothing about format; the preference persists
beside `adaptive_scrub` and `proxy_width`. The toggle must never read as a
filter — the graphs are computed from luma whatever the pane shows.
