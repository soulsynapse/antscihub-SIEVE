  The player now plays the frames a window render already decoded — a
  render-owned proxy ring fed from the executor's escaped source frame,
  consulted before every decode, with playback folding at the render's
  frontier while it fills — so the pane keeps moving during a render and
  the same file is no longer decoded twice.

decisions:
  - what: >
      `FrameResult.source` carries the decoded frame *before* the replicate
      crop, not the graph's cropped input, and is None when every root was
      served from the store.
    why: >
      The consumer this field exists for is a viewport that shows the whole
      frame, and a crop cannot be undone. None-on-warm-render is rule 6: a
      store-served result claiming a fresh decode would hand the ring pixels
      that were never read this pass.
  - what: >
      The ring (`gui/render_ring.py`) is render-owned, lock-guarded, luma-only,
      and bounded by a new ledger row (`RENDER_RING_SHARE`, 256 MB floor,
      fraction zero). A chroma frame is refused whole — no proxy, no frontier
      move. Proxies are made on the render thread at the player's proxy width.
    why: >
      Deliberately not `ProxyFrameCache` at the call site (playback must not
      evict a scrub's warmed grid — that module says why), though one sits
      inside for the LRU: the player loops the rendered prefix, and touching
      frames on the way past keeps the loop's span resident. The fraction is
      zero because growing the ring on big machines is the retention policy's
      question (docs/todo/proxy-retention-policy.md), not a side effect here.
  - what: >
      The player consults the ring before every decode, gated three ways —
      ring wired, `render_fed_playback` preference on (default), viewport
      gray — and folds playback at the render's frontier
      (`timeline_model.feed_bounds`) only while a window render is filling.
      After the render, the ring is still consulted but the fold lifts.
    why: >
      The luma gate is both format honesty (the ring's frames are gray, and a
      gray frame in a colour pane is the lie the gray-toggle item exists to
      prevent) and the tab scope for free, since the filter tab is the only
      place the pane goes gray. The fold is what makes a small ring
      sufficient: the playhead never runs ahead into frames whose only source
      would be the second decode this item removes. The 2026-07-27 luma
      finding inverted the original premise — the render (~88 fps) now
      outruns playback (59.94), so the steady state is "keep what the render
      produced and play it", and the frontier loop is the edge behaviour at
      render start and after a backward seek.
  - what: >
      The ring is cleared on the GUI thread inside `PreviewRunner.close()`
      after the revision bump, and again in the worker's queued `close`.
    why: >
      The ring is index-keyed with no source identity, so frames of a closed
      video must not survive into the next one's playback. The bump-then-clear
      ordering stops the abandoned render refilling what is being emptied
      (its next frame boundary raises before the put); the worker-side sweep
      catches the one frame that can slip past both in the same instant.

rejected:
  - what: Pacing the transport at the render's rate (playback silently at ~0.75x).
    why: >
      A transport lying about time — the item's own rejection, kept. The
      wall clock stays; what changes is where the frames come from and where
      the loop folds.
  - what: Converting chroma frames to luma in the ring, the day a graph needs chroma.
    why: >
      A conversion pass nobody measured, feeding a pane whose honest
      behaviour already exists: the player decodes for itself, as today.
