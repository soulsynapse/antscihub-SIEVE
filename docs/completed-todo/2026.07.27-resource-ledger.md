# Resource ledger

The one behavioural surprise worth recording beyond the diff: the cgroup
walk reads *ancestors*, not just the process's own cgroup, because a job
step's leaf often says `max` while the job-level parent holds the real
number — `test_an_ancestor_limit_binds_even_when_the_leaf_says_max` is the
pin. And the v1 unlimited sentinel (~2^63) is treated as "no limit", not as
an allocation, which is the fallthrough bug's mirror image.

The proxy-cache share is the one consumer that now *grows* with the machine
(1% of post-reserve, floored at the historical 96 MB); the two literals are
pinned equal by `tests/gui/test_proxy_cache.py` because the ledger cannot
import a QImage-bearing module without dragging Qt into its unit test.
