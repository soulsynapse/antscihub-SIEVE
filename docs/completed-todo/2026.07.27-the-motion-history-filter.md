summary: >
  The temporal chain can now hold a signal up after the animal stops producing
  it: `motion_history` is a leaky accumulator in physical units with optional
  neighbourhood coupling, and it declares the group delay that causality costs
  it rather than leaving onsets biased late by an unwritten amount.

decisions:
  - what: >
      Group delay is *declared* — `group_delay()` in frames, and
      `MotionHistoryParams.group_delay_frames()`/`.group_delay_seconds()` — as a
      module function with a test, not as a `FilterSpec` field.
    why: >
      The item permitted declaring or removing and forbade neither. Removing it
      means running the accumulator forward and backward, which needs the whole
      record and therefore `Mode.WINDOWED` and a kernel protocol that does not
      exist (`docs/todo/kernel-protocol-beyond-one-frame.md`), so declaring was
      the only reachable half. It is not a spec field because nothing reads one:
      a contract field no consumer consumes is a number, and the day the
      detection chain corrects an onset is the day it earns promotion.
  - what: >
      The source term is half-wave rectified — `s[t] = max(input, 0)` — and
      weighted by `(1 - lambda)` rather than saturating at `tau`.
    why: >
      Both keep the output in the input's units, which is what lets a threshold
      in `temporal_baseline` deviations still mean deviations downstream. MHI's
      saturation puts the output in units of *time*, which nothing in the
      detection chain compares against anything, and a signed source lets
      `dilate` propagate the least-negative value outward and a lull cancel a
      bout that really happened.
  - what: >
      `reach_blocks` is realized per mode — `kappa = exp(-1/reach)` for
      `dilate`, `D = reach^2/(2 tau)` for `diffuse` — and `diffuse` sub-steps to
      hold the explicit scheme under a diffusion number of 1/4.
    why: >
      Sub-stepping was taken over refusing the unstable combination because the
      constraint couples three parameters, two of which the user is not thinking
      about while dragging the third; a validator would land as a slider that
      stops responding, where sub-stepping lands as a number in the benchmark
      readout the user is already watching.

rejected:
  - what: A separate `emit=energy` mode returning the binarized support (MEI).
    why: >
      Binarizing is a threshold, and `core/detection.py` is where this repo puts
      thresholds. A second one here would be a second place to set the same
      number, and the two would disagree.
  - what: A Gaussian blur as the `diffuse` coupling, which is the obvious spelling.
    why: >
      `cv2.GaussianBlur` truncates its kernel, and the per-step sigma this
      filter needs is well under one block at ordinary settings — the kernel
      collapses to the identity and the diffusion silently never happens. The
      five-point stencil has no such floor: its coefficient is exactly
      `D dt` however small that is.
