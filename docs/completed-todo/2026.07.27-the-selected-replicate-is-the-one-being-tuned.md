# ---- the load-bearing part ------------------------------------------------
decisions:
  - what: the selection lives on ReplicateDocument, not on either tab
    why: two views each keeping an answer to "which arena am I looking at" is
      the failure the transport had before the timeline replaced it — and the
      table cannot be the authority anyway, because ReplicateTableModel resets
      wholesale on structure_changed and a reset clears the view's selection
  - what: "removal above the selection shifts silently; of it, reselects and emits"
    why: "`selection_changed` drives a re-render, so a removal that merely
      renumbers the selected row must not clear the HUD and re-render the same
      arena; views that track the row resync on `structure_changed` instead"
  - what: table-row click selects, video-box click accepts and navigates
    why: the vision's "left click accepts" read literally leaves no way to
      select while drawing the next twelve; splitting the gestures keeps both,
      and mirrors the filter tab's click-to-navigate on its own video
  - what: no deselection gesture, and select() refuses nonsense rows
    why: '"no replicate selected" is not a state the tuning loop has a
      rendering for while replicates exist — an empty-space click clearing the
      selection would flip the preview to an uncropped 47.6 MB/frame render'
  - what: accepting is a submit, not a job
    why: the crop is applied per frame at the graph's root by the executor
      (docs/findings/2026.07.25-the-crop-belongs-in-the-graph.md); a
      selection change resubmits through PreviewRunner.request_render and
      invalidates exactly as a window move does
  - what: selection is not undoable
    why: set_pipeline's reason — it changes nothing a save writes, and a
      Ctrl+Z hopping the viewpoint around between real edits undoes things the
      user never thinks of as edits

rejected:
  - what: emitting selection_changed for row-number shifts
    why: the filter tab re-renders on that signal; a shift re-rendering the
      identical arena would erase the HUD's series for nothing
  - what: keeping the tab's replicate_added-selects-row connection
    why: apply_insert selecting its own row moves the behaviour to where
      redo also gets it, and one owner means one place the rule lives
