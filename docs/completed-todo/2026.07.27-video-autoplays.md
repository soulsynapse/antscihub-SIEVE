  An opened video starts playing by itself. The call sits at the very end of

  the transport never runs over an unbound document or behind an unanswered
  modal.

decisions:
  - what: >
      Autoplay is the last statement in `_on_opened`, after the branch, not
      inside either arm of it.
    why: >
      Both surviving constraints are ordering ones and both fail silently.
      Above `bind_source` the transport advances over a document with no
      source; above `_offer_neighbour_project` it advances behind a blocking
      modal. Placing it after the branch satisfies both at once and costs one
      `return` converted to an `else`.
  - what: >
      The guard is `self._play_action.isEnabled()`, not a fresh
      `metadata is not None`.
    why: >
      The item asked for the already-written-down "is there a video" test, and
      the action carries it — plus the editing one from
      `_on_editor_open_changed`. The stronger reading is the right one: if play
      is not being *offered*, it should not happen unasked either.
  - what: >
      `restore_last_video` autoplays too, so a session that remembers a video
      comes up playing.
    why: >
      It reaches the transport through `open_video` like every other caller.
      Carving out an exception would mean a second rule about which opens count
      as opens, and the item's sentence is unqualified: an opened video should
      start playing.

rejected:
  - what: A separate autoplay branch inside `_adopt_project`'s arm and the neighbour arm.
    why: >
      Two call sites for one behaviour, and the neighbour arm's copy would have
      to be threaded past a modal whose answer can itself open a project. One
      site after the branch is the same behaviour with nothing to keep in step.
