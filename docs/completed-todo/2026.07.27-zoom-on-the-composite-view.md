decisions:
  - what: the magnifier moved to `gui/zoom.py` and both widgets hold one,
      rather than the composite pane growing its own zoom and pan
    why: the item said the clamp is the part that costs a day, and a second
      copy of it is how two views end up disagreeing about what the floor
      means; the extraction is behaviour-preserving on the replicate side —
      all 69 of its existing tests pass untouched, which is what makes it a
      move rather than a rewrite
  - what: the shared centre speaks in normalized content coordinates, not
      source pixels
    why: what the two views share is not a coordinate system but the rule for
      producing one — `VideoView` maps to source pixels, the pane maps to
      blocks, and a pane showing a block grid with no frame has no pixel
      dimensions at all, so a helper denominated in source pixels would have
      had nothing to say to it
  - what: images and grid both paint into `view_rect`, and `grid_rect` *is*
      `view_rect`
    why: registration between a cell and the pixels it describes then cannot
      come apart, rather than being maintained in two places that agree today
  - what: "`block_at` tests containment twice — the grid rect and the fit"
    why: a magnified grid extends under the letterbox, where paint is clipped
      away; answering there would solo a cell from a strip of bare panel,
      which is rule 6's mirror direction — a control answering where nothing
      is drawn
  - what: the header states the magnification while it is above the fit
    why: 8x on a grid shows a handful of cells that look like the whole thing,
      and magnified is a state a user can forget they are in

rejected:
  - what: a double-click or right-click to return to the fit
    why: the pane's left click already solos and Qt sends a press before a
      double-click, so the reset would arrive having already toggled a block;
      scrolling out lands *exactly* on the fit by the clamp, so the gesture
      would buy convenience at the cost of the one unambiguous click the
      grid has
  - what: resetting the zoom when the selected replicate changes
    why: the clamp guarantees the view never shows anything outside the
      picture, so a carried magnification is disorienting at worst and
      useful at best; a new *source* is a new picture and does reset, which
      is the rule the replicate viewport already keeps
