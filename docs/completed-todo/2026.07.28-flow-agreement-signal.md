# flow_agreement, the one free measure the screening promoted

The three tests each fail for a distinct real reason, and the third is the one
that pins the normalisation choice: with half the block featureless, 57.7% of
its pixels resolve a flow, so the rejected block-count normalisation reads
~0.50 where the shipped one reads ~0.87. The threshold sits at 0.8 so that the
wrong denominator fails rather than merely shifting the number.

`test_static_input_is_exactly_zero_for_every_signal` already iterates the
enum, so the new signal inherited the zero-change guard's coverage for free —
which is why the second test targets the *aperture*-degenerate case instead,
where the danger is the opposite lie: an empty circular mean treated as
perfect agreement and rendered as a confident 1.

Outside the item's scope: `test_every_signal_has_a_label_on_the_quick_switch`
asserts `SIGNAL_LABELS` covers `Signal` exhaustively. Adding the entry made it
obvious that a signal the kernel computes but the switcher cannot name would
be reachable only by hand-editing a saved file, and nothing was checking.
