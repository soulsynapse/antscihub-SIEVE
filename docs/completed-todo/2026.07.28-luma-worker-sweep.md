summary: >
  The luma decode path infers two workers instead of four, because two is now
  where its curve peaks and four is 21% past it — measured rather than inherited,
  which is what the constant's comment had been promising since it was written.

decisions:
  - what: >
      a second constant, `LUMA_WORKER_CAP = 2`, rather than a formula relating
      the two caps or a scale factor off the frame size
    why: >
      the colour cap's reason is a 47.6 MB buffer and this one's is that
      declining the convert left almost no parallel work — a formula would
      assert a mechanism the measurement refutes, since a 3x smaller buffer
      moved the optimum *down*. Two independent numbers with two curves behind
      them cannot be wrong together in a way nobody notices.
  - what: >
      `resolve_workers(requested, *, luma=False)` — a keyword on the one
      resolver, not a second resolver and not a `PrefetchFrameSource` method
    why: >
      the module's own rule is that one function decides a count when the
      caller did not. A `resolve_luma_workers` would be the second answer that
      docstring spends a paragraph forbidding, and a caller could pick the
      wrong name for the format it opened. Defaulting to colour keeps a caller
      that has not been taught to pass it slow rather than wrong, matching
      `cli/common.frame_source`.
  - what: >
      two workers rather than three
    why: >
      two wins the median of three runs and two of the three individually,
      holds a third less in-flight memory, and is already what
      `PREVIEW_WORKERS` declares. Three was defensible on run one alone and is
      recorded in the finding as the runner-up.
  - what: >
      split H2 out of `docs/todo/ledger-measurements.md` and defer the rest
    why: >
      H2 needed the reference footage and a headless process; H3 and H4 need an
      instrumented session with a person tuning in it, which is also the trigger
      `docs/todo/proxy-retention-policy.md` waits on. Keeping them in one item
      would have made a measurement nobody could take block one anybody could.

rejected:
  - what: >
      taking H3's RSS floor under `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen` while the
      machinery was out anyway
    why: >
      an offscreen process has no swapchain, no driver allocation, and no
      decoder warmed by a real scrub, so the number would look measured and be
      worse-founded than the formula it replaced. Rule 6 in the direction that
      is easy to miss — a guess labelled a guess beats a measurement of the
      wrong thing. Written into the deferred item rather than left as an
      omission.
  - what: >
      scaling `INFERRED_WORKER_CAP` by the buffer ratio (47.6 / 15.9) to get a
      luma cap of twelve
    why: >
      that is the prediction the sweep was run to test, and twelve workers
      measured 20.04 ms/frame against a sequential 8.49 — 2.4x worse. It is in
      the finding's `closed` list because the reasoning behind it, not just the
      number, was wrong.
