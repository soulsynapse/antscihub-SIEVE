summary: >
  The rework became drivable by a fresh session: REWORK.md gained a Gate line
  per rule, its open questions closed as dated decisions (channel not
  intervals; presentation on the spec, visibly non-hashed; incremental ratchet
  on main), a fourth ordering constraint (parity before the migration), and a
  graduation mechanism; CLAUDE.md routes to it ahead of pre-rework items and
  settled rows; the work landed as twenty-six todo items wired with `after:`
  edges; and the doc machinery grew the two moves the rework needs — a
  `superseded` status that keeps absorbed items' files while removing them
  from every takeable list, and an `overturns:` key that lets a completing
  entry retire a settled row without editing the dated record that made it.

settled:
  - what: Absorbed items are superseded, never completed or deferred
    where: "`tools/doc_index.py`"
    do_not_redecide: >
      `status: superseded` plus `superseded_by:` (slugs, resolved like
      `after:` and gate-checked) keeps the file so inbound references —
      including ones in `src/` — keep resolving, and drops the item from the
      open, deferred, frontier, and aspiration lists. Completing absorbed
      scope puts unfinished work in the Completed index (rule 6 arriving
      through the docs); deferring it misdescribes scope as timing.
  - what: A settled row is overturned by the entry that re-decides it, by name
    where: "`tools/doc_index.py`"
    do_not_redecide: >
      `overturns:` on a completed entry matches a row's `what` exactly
      (a typo fails the suite) and moves it to SETTLED.md's Overturned
      section carrying both pointers. The original entry is a dated record
      and is never edited — and a row stands as law until the overturning
      entry actually lands, so rows the rework will eventually re-decide are
      not pre-struck.
  - what: A REWORK rule graduates through its Gate line, by hand
    where: "`docs/REWORK.md`"
    do_not_redecide: >
      A rule leaves REWORK.md only when its Gate names something that exists
      and the shrink-only list behind it is empty; the sentence folds into
      the ARCHITECTURE.md rule it strengthens through that rule's own
      falsifier process. Nothing automates the fold — a tool that edits prose
      in two documents is a churn machine — and when the last rule graduates,
      REWORK.md flips to `record`.
