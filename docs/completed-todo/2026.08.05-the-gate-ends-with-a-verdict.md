summary: >
  `nox -s checks` now ends by writing one line to stdout — `checks: pass` or
  `checks: FAIL (<stage>)` — so a reader who tails the gate's output, which
  is the only way it gets read at thousands of lines, learns whether it
  passed and which of the four tools said otherwise.
