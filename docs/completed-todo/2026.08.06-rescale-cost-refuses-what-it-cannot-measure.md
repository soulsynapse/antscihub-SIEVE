summary: >
  The rescale cost fit now refuses instead of reporting a curve it cannot
  support: samples carry PreviewRender.reuse and a spread across cache
  regimes does not fit, a negative fixed floor or negative scale-sensitive
  term is refused rather than clamped to zero, and a third distinct scale
  gates a residual check. Each refusal carries its own reason for the label
  and tooltip. A two-scale fit is exactly determined, so it is reported with
  an estimate mark rather than as a tested claim.

settled:
  - what: Rescale cost samples are comparable only within one cache regime
    where: "`src/sieve/gui/rescale_cost.py`, `src/sieve/gui/filter_tab.py`"

      A sample carries PreviewRender.reuse and a fit refuses when the spread
      exceeds REUSE_SPREAD_LIMIT. Do not pool warm and cold renders, and do
      not clamp a negative F or M into a reportable curve — the probe that
      produced the model ran every scale cold on purpose.
  - what: A two-scale rescale fit is reported but not validated
    where: "`src/sieve/gui/rescale_cost.py` RescaleCostFit.validated"
    do_not_redecide: >
      An exactly-determined fit is shown with the estimate mark rather than
      withheld; clearing the mark requires a third distinct scale and a
      residual passing MIN_FIT_QUALITY. Do not raise the reporting threshold
      to three scales, and do not present two as tested.
