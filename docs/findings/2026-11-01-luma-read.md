# Gray from the decoder's luma plane: what the YUV-to-BGR conversion was costing

2026-11-01, on v5-blob-detection over 8a08c367, cv2 5.0.0 with its prebuilt FFmpeg plugin, Windows.
Steady-state reads through `Decoder`, 40 consecutive rows after the first, one run each; `step_ms` is
the decoder's own running mean.

## The GoPro clip

`video-tests/GX010047c2_02_17_26.MP4`, HEVC 5312×2988, pixel format yuvj420p, reported by cv2 as I420.

| output | path | a row | step_ms | first row |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bgr | decode, swscale to BGR | 16.9 ms | | |
| gray | bgr + cvtColor BGR2GRAY | 19.5 ms | 23.2 | 326 ms |
| gray | luma plane, CAP_PROP_CONVERT_RGB off | 9.0 ms | 15.7 | 286 ms |

cvtColor alone is 2.4 ms on this frame; the rest of the 10 ms gained is the YUV-to-BGR conversion
the decode no longer pays. The luma plane and the converted gray differ by 0.8 of a level on average,
standard deviation 0.4, on this full-range clip; on the limited-range h264 clip
`rep3_intermittent_crop.MP4` (462×456) the mean difference is 1.3 with a standard deviation of 4.1
and extremes of ±17 on saturated colour, since cvtColor applies BT.601 weights to the expanded BGR
and the luma carries the stream's own matrix. A threshold tuned on one may shift a few levels on the
other. The small clip reads in 0.12 ms either way; the conversion only shows at size.

## What cv2 does with conversion off

With `CAP_PROP_CONVERT_RGB` 0 the FFmpeg plugin hands out plane 0 of the decoded picture as a
rows × cols 8UC1 array for any format it does not list, which includes yuv420p and yuvj420p, and
warns on every such frame. The plugin takes its log level from `OPENCV_LOG_LEVEL` in the process's
launch environment only: setting the variable in-process before `import cv2`, through `os.environ`,
`putenv` or `SetEnvironmentVariableW`, and `cv2.utils.logging.setLogLevel`, all leave the warning
printing, though the latter does set the host library's level. The player therefore sends C-level
stderr to nowhere at startup and re-points `sys.stderr` to the kept handle, which also silences
libavcodec's reference-picture errors on seeks. `SIEVE_C_STDERR=1` keeps C-level stderr.

The decoder takes the luma path only for I420 and only when the plugin is quiet; any other stream,
or a `Decoder` used outside the player without `OPENCV_LOG_LEVEL` set, converts from BGR as before.

## Range

cv2 reports I420 for yuv420p and yuvj420p alike, so the stream's range is measured at open instead:
row 0 is decoded both ways and the BGR conversion, which knows the range, is fitted against the
plane. The slope is 1.165 to 1.181 on the Pi h264 files and the small h264 clip, limited range, and
0.999 on the GoPro yuvj420p clip, full range. A limited plane is expanded through a lookup table,
`(v - 16) * 255 / 219`, so the video node's gray spans 0 to 255 whichever path produced it. A row
too flat to fit, variance under 25, is taken as limited. The measurement is made at the first gray
read, not at open, since on the 5.3K clip the two decodes of row 0 it needs cost about 0.6 s. The
video node's third output, luma, is the plane as is, no table, for whoever wants the stream's own
values; the pane says which range it is in.
