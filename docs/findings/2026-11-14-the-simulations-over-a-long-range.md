# Over 2048 rows every class judged under way is found before its walk's last row, `waits` fires beside `signals` in two runs of seven, and a held rewalk reads as `grows`

2026-11-14, on v5-cleaning-up-monitor over ce86e5a3 with `ledger.SETTLES_BY` and the simulations' `--long`
in the working tree, by `.venv\Scripts\python -m sieve_player.dev.simulations <names> --long`, offscreen.
Whether the machine was otherwise in use was not noted. It is the check of the three boxes of
`docs/spec/monitor.md`'s A walk is judged while it is under way that ask for a class found before a
walk's last row, and the sighting behind the two boxes added under them.

`--long` writes each simulation a clip of `simulations.LONG` rows where it writes 64, the fault the same
a row: the memo's limit is scaled to the range and pile's ms a row is scaled down by it, so each is the
share of its walk it is over 64. A walk then runs over 5 to 50 buckets where it ran over one or two. An
entry's first sighting is its walk's last progress as it was judged, so one strictly inside a walk was
found with that walk short of its range, and the command's exit holds each fault to that.

## `waits` beside `signals`

In two of the seven runs of `signals` the log also held `waits` on the same slot, found at the walk's end
and not under way: the tools 112 and 114 ms off their CPU while the GUI thread was in its spans, 11% of
what the walk's nodes cost, against `catalogue.WAITS` at a tenth. Over 64 rows the same wait is a few ms,
under `catalogue.TICK`, and the class reads none of it. `signals` leaves a slot longer than `BRIEF` a call
to `waits` by its own rule; `waits` has no rule that leaves a brief one to `signals`, and its docstring's
"the interpreter changing hands at every call lands in the tools' spans" is the wait it then reads. One
fault is two entries at a share that sits on the threshold, so some runs and not others.

## `grows` on a held rewalk

The healthy `rewalk`, the clip walked twice with the memo holding the first, surfaced `grows` on pile in
four runs of four, in its second walk: 0.02 to 0.07 ms a run over the last quarter against 0.01 over the
first, 48 to 63% of what the walk's nodes cost and 0.00 to 0.01 ms a row, the walk 0.25 to 0.30 s for its
2048 rows. It was found under way in three and at the walk's end in one, so it is not of judging under
way. pile is run whole at every emit and converts the rows so far to an array, which costs by their
count with its `ms` at nothing; `GROWS_SHARE` is a share of what the walk's nodes cost, and in a walk
the stores held that is next to nothing, some 10 ms of a quarter second of wall. Over 64 rows the
conversion is too short to show. It is a sighting for the fork on healthy documents, on the
simulations' own graph.

## After the two were fixed

The same day, in the same working tree: `waits` reads no slot whose calls in a bucket took `BRIEF` ms
each or under, and `GROWS_SHARE` is of the walk's nodes' ms with its unprobed. `signals` then surfaced
its class alone in ten runs of ten, `rewalk` nothing in four of four, the other seven healthy runs
nothing, one run each, and `memo`, `cache`, `reader`, `seeks`, `slot`, `unread` and `grows` each its
class alone and under way, one run each, `grows` still at 19% of its nodes' cost.
