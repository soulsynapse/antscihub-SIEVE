---
title: The decode format is free to derive
date: 2026-07-28
status: closed
tags: [decode, cache-key, gui, render-thread]

verdict: >
  `graph_needs_chroma` over a three-node chain costs a median of 8.9 us, p95
  13.2 us, max 77.5 us. The render thread called it twice per render, inside
  `full_preview_render` — 18 us against a span measured in seconds. The cost
  half of docs/todo/the-decode-format-has-six-derivations.md is not a reason to
  do anything, and the item said to find that out before assuming otherwise.

question: >
  docs/todo/the-decode-format-has-six-derivations.md asked for the number
  before the change: `graph_needs_chroma` is `Dag.build(...).needs_chroma`, a
  full graph build with parameter validation, run twice per render on the
  render thread inside a timed span, and whether that was microseconds or
  milliseconds was recorded nowhere.

source:
  build: Python 3.11, this tree at 14ce201
  machine: the reference workstation (32 cpus, 68.4 GB available, Windows 11)
  method: >
    2000 calls after 50 warm-up calls, `time.perf_counter` around each, over
    rescale -> normalize -> block_signal with the real shelf. No caching exists
    anywhere on the path — checked, because a memoized `Dag.build` would have
    made the number meaningless.

measurements:
  - probe: graph_needs_chroma, 3-node chain, 2000 calls
    result: median 8.9 us, p95 13.2 us, max 77.5 us
  - probe: what the render thread paid per render (two calls)
    result: ~18 us, inside a `full_preview_render` budget of 3000 ms

consequences:
  - >
    The correctness argument for a single home stands entirely on its own, and
    the change made was made on it: six sites deriving the format are six
    chances of being handed a different graph, not six chances of being slow.
    `RenderRequest.luma` and `ExecutionPlan.luma` exist for that reason; the
    `Dag` build leaving the render thread is a side effect worth 18 us.
  - >
    A cost claim about this path should not be made again without re-measuring.
    The number is small enough that any future argument for restructuring here
    has to be a correctness argument.

closed:
  - what: moving the derivation off the render thread for throughput
    why: 18 us per render, six orders of magnitude under the span it sits in

open_questions:
  - >
    Three nodes. A graph an order of magnitude larger has not been measured,
    and `Dag.build` is linear-ish in nodes plus a pydantic validation per node,
    so the number scales with the graph rather than being constant. Nothing on
    today's shelf produces such a graph.
