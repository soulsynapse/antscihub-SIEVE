# The freeze during a render is the fold, not the detector

The chain is three connections. `main_window.py:367` wires
`PreviewRunner.window_render_changed` to `VideoPlayer.set_render_filling`, and
`filter_tab.py:409` wires the same signal to the auto-gray toggle, which turns
`_viewport_luma` on and so opens the third of `_feed_ring`'s three gates. Both
conditions at `player.py:426` therefore become true on the same emission: the
instant a window render starts, `timerEvent` replaces the playback bounds with
`feed_bounds(bounds, ring.frontier)`.

`feed_bounds` returns `ClipRange(start=window.start, end=min(window.end,
frontier + 1))`. The frontier is "the last frame the render has produced", reset
when a window render starts, so for the whole first stretch of a render it sits
at or just past `window.start`. The span is then one or two frames wide and
`playback_step` loops inside it — the playhead advances at whatever rate the
chain emits frames, which on an expensive chain is a stall or a two-frame
shuffle. When the render ends, `window_render_changed(False)` clears
`_render_filling`, the fold lifts, and playback snaps back to the wall clock.
That is the reported symptom in full, including why it ends when the graphs
finish.

**The guard that is missing is the one the docstring already argues for.**
`feed_bounds` returns the window unchanged for a `None` frontier and for a
frontier behind the window, and says why: a fold that "pinned playback to a
single frame, or to a foreign span, would freeze the pane in the name of
keeping it moving." A frontier of exactly `window.start` produces `end =
start + 1` — a single frame — and is not guarded. The function states the
failure mode it permits. That is worth recording separately from the fix,
because it is the kind of defect a reader of the module cannot see: the
docstring reads as though the case were handled.

## Why the recommendation is to delete rather than tune

The obvious repair is a minimum lead — fold only once the frontier covers, say,
a second of frames. It buys back most of the bandwidth for most of a render and
costs a tuning constant, which is the shape this repo refuses elsewhere and
which would need a producer under rule 4 to be a real number rather than a
guess.

The alternative is to drop `set_render_filling`, the
`window_render_changed` → player connection, and `feed_bounds` entirely, and
let the ring be purely a cache: a hit for frames the render has reached, an
ordinary decode for the rest. The fold's purpose — not decoding frames the
render has not produced — is a decode-bandwidth argument, and the symptom is
the evidence that the trade is backwards at the moment it matters most, which
is while somebody is watching to decide whether the tune is right. The
preference stays as the lever for anyone who wants the bandwidth back.

What would overturn this: the confirmation gesture above coming back with a
picture that still freezes with the preference off. Then the fold is at most
half of it and `FilterTab._apply`'s per-partial repaint is the other half,
which is a different fix in a different file.
