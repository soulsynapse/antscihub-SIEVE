# Every contract goes red on one line, uninstalled packages included

The interesting case is `gui -> sieve.tools`. It is legal under the layers
contract — `gui` sits above `tools`, so the import points downward — and it is
the reason `gui-computes-nothing` exists as a separate forbidden contract
rather than as another layer line. If the proof had shown that edge caught by
the layers contract, the extra contract would have been redundant; it was not.

What a failure would have looked like: grimp reporting `Analyzed 12 files, 0
dependencies` on the violation branch, or the two external contracts staying
KEPT while the three internal ones broke. Neither happened.

The `unmatched_ignore_imports_alerting = error` setting on
`gui-computes-nothing` is untested here and untestable while the exception list
is empty — it only fires on a stale entry, and there are no entries. It is
carried on v2's evidence, where it is what made that list shrink-only.
