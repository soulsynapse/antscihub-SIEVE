# The dilation radius is the one motion_history quantity no case reaches

The first sweep reported a fourth survivor — `mode="edge"` to `mode="constant"`
in `_diffuse` — and it was an artifact of the anchor, which matches the comment
one line above the call before it matches the call. Re-anchored on the whole
`np.pad(...)` expression the mutant dies to the diffuse golden, exactly as the
boundary comment says it should. That is
[a mutant anchored on a token that also appears in prose reports a false survivor](loop/2026.08.07-a-mutant-anchored-on-a-token-that-also-appears-in-prose-reports-a-false-survivor.md)
recurring in a reviewer that had read the finding, which is worth knowing about
how much protection reading it buys.

What would have refuted the radius gap: any `dilate` case at a persistence short
enough, or a reach large enough, that `reach_blocks / (tau_seconds * fps)`
exceeds one. There is none. The quantity is not decorative — it is how many
blocks the dilation reaches per frame, chosen so activity travels `reach_blocks`
over one `tau`, and a wrong one produces a plausible bloom of the wrong width.
`test_the_bloom_stops_growing` asserts the support stops growing and stays under
`SIDE * SIDE // 3`; at the reach it runs, both hold whether the radius is the
formula's or a constant 1, so the existing case is not a weak check of the
formula — it is not a check of it at all.

The measured corner also says why the missing case is not a one-line
parametrize. The arena and the `SIDE * SIDE // 3` guard were both sized for
`reach_blocks = 2`, and every configuration that puts the radius above its clamp
blooms past that guard — 913 cells at reach 4, the whole arena at reach 16 —
while still stopping, which is the tool behaving correctly at a reach the
constant beside `REACH_BLOCKS_MAX` already calls "past where neighbours still
means one animal". A case that pins the radius has to carry its own geometry.
