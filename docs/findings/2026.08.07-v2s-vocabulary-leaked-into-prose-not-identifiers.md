# v2's vocabulary leaked into prose, never into an identifier

`clip_start` is the interesting one. `core/tool_base.py` was written after
ADR-15 settled, by someone re-deriving a warmup contract rather than porting
one, and the name still came over — in the sentence explaining what the number
is *for*, where naming the thing you are measuring against is exactly when you
reach for the vocabulary you have. That is the failure mode the gate is shaped
around: not a copied file, but a copied word in the one place no other test
looks.

A prediction the result would have flipped on: had the scan found a `clip`
field or a `roi` argument in `core/pipeline_model.py`, schema v1 would have
been a port wearing a re-derivation's docstring, and the fix would have been
the model rather than two lines of prose. It found neither, which is the
positive half of this — the boundary held where it was load-bearing.
