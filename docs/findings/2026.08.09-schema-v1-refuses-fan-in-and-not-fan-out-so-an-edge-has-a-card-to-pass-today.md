# Schema v1 refuses fan-in and not fan-out, so an edge already has a card to pass

The argument that put the painter last in Phase 9 was that occlusion has no
subject until a node has two inputs. It reads as one claim and is two: a *card
to pass behind* needs an edge whose ends are not adjacent in the walk, and that
is a fan-out, while a *port to name* needs an edge arriving beside another one,
which is the fan-in schema v1 refuses. Only the second waits on 09.2.

What would have made the argument right is a schema that refused branching in
either direction, or a walk that emitted a node's children before their
subtrees — under a breadth-first order n1 and n2 would be adjacent to n0 and no
edge would span anything. `walk.py` is depth-first and says so, for a reason of
its own (a chain reads down the page), and that is what puts a card in the way.

The consequence for the plan is narrow: the painter still comes after the cards,
because it is read off their geometry. It simply did not have to come after the
output step.
