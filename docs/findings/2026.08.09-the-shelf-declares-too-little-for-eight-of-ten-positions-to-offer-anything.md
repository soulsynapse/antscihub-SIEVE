# The shelf declares too little for eight of ten positions to offer anything

The prediction, had the ruling been wrong, was that `matches` would behave like
`admits` with a smaller constant — a shortlist of six or seven instead of ten,
useless for the same reason. It does not: the distribution is bimodal, 2 or 0,
and which of the two a position gets is decided entirely by whether its
upstream's `emits` states `channels`. That is the predicate discriminating on
exactly what it was designed to discriminate on, and it is why the eight zeros
are a fact about the shelf rather than about the comparison.

The number to watch is the count of tools whose `emits` leaves a field empty.
Eight of ten today; every one of them is a preserving or dtype-agnostic tool,
so it will not fall by tools being more careful — it falls when something
resolves the stream fields along the walk, or not at all.
