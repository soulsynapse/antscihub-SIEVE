# A params-dependent fold cannot live on the Dag

ADR 37 was written the day the axis half was built and it reused the shape
`elements` already had — declare absolutely or relative to the input, fold
forward on the `Dag`, `None` refuses. Three of those four transfer. The fold
does not, and the reason is not an implementation detail: `elements` answers a
question about *kinds*, which a document fixes, while an axis answers a question
about *numbers*, which a replicate can move.

What would refute this: a `Dag` that already carried a replicate, or an axis
declaration that could not be a function of the params. Neither holds —
`Dag.build` takes a `Pipeline`, and the function form is the one form the
`element` declaration never needed and the axis could not do without, since a
Morlet bank is not knowable until `fps` is set.

The near answer worth naming, so a later session does not re-derive it: a
`Dag` rebuilt per replicate would restore the ADR's placement literally. It is
refused here on cost rather than on principle — the tuning loop's whole
constraint is that a slider drag refills faster than the video plays, and a
graph rebuild per deviating region sits directly on that path. That is a
measurement nobody has taken, so it is an open question and not a closed one.
