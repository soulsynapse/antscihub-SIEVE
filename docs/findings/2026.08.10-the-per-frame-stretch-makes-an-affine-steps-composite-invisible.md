# The canvas's per-frame stretch makes an affine step's composite invisible

`canvas.py`'s docstring already states that a node's output has no display
range and so is stretched between that frame's own extremes, and gives the
reason: a fixed range renders black every tool whose units are not already
0..1. What that costs was stated there as brightness not being comparable
across *frames*. The composite makes it cost one thing more, across *nodes*:
two layers each stretched through their own extremes are the same picture
whenever one is an affine map of the other, so the blend that is supposed to
show what the step did shows nothing at all.

This is not a defect in the composite and it is not a reason to fix the
stretch here. It is the boundary of what the surface can say, and it was found
the only way it could be — by writing the case and watching two grabs that
should have differed compare equal.
