# A mechanism disabled one at a time is reported against the criterion it does not reach

The failure is in the reporting, not the testing — all three mechanisms are
covered somewhere in the suite, so nothing here was shipped untested. What was
lost is the information a reviewer uses to decide where to look. "Disabling any
of the three turns it red" says the criterion is a sufficient gate on all three,
and a reviewer who takes that at face value has no reason to re-derive the
boundary of the one mechanism the criterion is silent about.

Restating it as node ids costs the worker nothing and would have read: two
mechanisms killed by `a_bounded_warmup_tool_served_from_the_store_equals_its_cold_run`,
one killed by two tests written in the same commit and by no criterion. That
second line is a reviewer's whole reading list.
