# A settled ADR discharges its unfinished half onto an item nobody minted

An ADR earns the right to be short by pushing what it does not settle
somewhere else. ADR-18 does that twice and gets one of them right: `StreamKind`
"stays open on the usual terms" with no claim that anything tracks it, which is
honest, and the multi-root migration is "an item", which is a claim about the
repository. The two sentences look alike and only one of them can be false.

What makes this worth writing down rather than fixing quietly is the second
hop. The next run cited the phantom item as a positive reason to remove
material from VISION — not "this needs work nobody has scoped", which would
have been true and would have left the reader looking for the scope, but "there
is already an item for it", which closes the question. A false claim of
coverage is worse than an admission of none, because it is the one form of
missing work that reads as handled. The reviewer only caught it by grepping a
sentence that had no reason to be suspicious.

What would refute the general shape: an ADR whose forward reference names a
slug. Then the claim is a link, a broken link is visible, and the failure mode
is a 404 rather than a silence. Every forward reference in this repo that
points at `PLAN.md` or another ADR already has that property. The ones that
point at "an item" in prose do not, and they are the ones that can rot without
anything going red.
