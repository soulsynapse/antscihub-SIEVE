# A test named for a semantic is the one nobody mutates

The run under review did the verification it was asked for and did it honestly.
It wrote the tests first, showed them red, took its goldens from v2's own
package rather than from its own output — this review regenerated all three out
of v2 at `main` in a separate process and they are byte-identical — and it ran
two mutants that both died. Every sentence of its account is true.

The two mutants are the tell. `INTER_LINEAR` for `INTER_AREA` and pooled
statistics for the gray projection are precisely the two decisions the two
module docstrings spend paragraphs defending. A worker that has just written
those paragraphs is primed to test exactly them, and the priming is the
enumeration: what got mutated is what the run had recently argued about.
Rounding is not argued about anywhere, because it is not contentious — it is
just v1's semantic, transcribed. Uncontentious is the same thing as unexamined.

This sharpens
[a mutation sweep enumerated by hand misses what it did not look for](2026.08.07-a-mutation-sweep-enumerated-by-hand-misses-what-it-did-not-look-for.md).
There the enumeration was narrowed by the *reopening* — the run was sent after
refusals and enumerated refusals. Here nothing narrowed it externally; the
narrowing came from the run's own writing, which is available in every item and
not just reopened ones. And it sharpens
[reverting the implementation is no proof when the item created the module](2026.08.07-reverting-the-implementation-is-no-proof-when-the-item-created-the-module.md)
in the direction that finding pointed: mutating is the right instrument, and
the reviewer generating its own list from the source is the half that matters,
because the worker's list is a picture of what the worker was thinking about.

Two of the six survivors were the instrument lying, which is its own finding —
see
[a mutant anchored on a token that also appears in prose reports a false survivor](2026.08.07-a-mutant-anchored-on-a-token-that-also-appears-in-prose-reports-a-false-survivor.md).

The prediction that would refute the framing: if the rounding gap had been one
the run named and dismissed with a reason, this would be a scoping call rather
than a blind spot. The commit message names two mutants as though two were the
set, and the test's own comment asserts the third semantic as fact.
