A smaller pane anchored to a side inside a main pane. It is a
[pane](pane.md) in every other respect; what the word records is the
anchoring, not a different kind of space.

## Where it lives

`gui/frame/panes.py`, which names the sides a subpane may anchor to in each of
the three. A strip that is not anchored to a side of a pane is not one.
