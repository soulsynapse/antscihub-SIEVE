A run of views laid side by side inside one pane, one of them in front at a time, reached by sliding the run rather than by replacing what the pane holds. A swipe is itself a view: it occupies one pane and never adds one, so a screen reached by swiping is a position on the track and not a pane of its own.

The word covers the track and every position on it. The right pane's swipe is
the three: the project, the pipeline, the step. Their order is the reading order
of the work, which is what lets ← and → mean out and in without a label saying
so.

"Pane" is not a synonym here even though the mockup's own names use it — a pane
is the space the window divides into, and those are countable and fixed, so a
construct that grew two more of them each time a screen was added would make
"which pane" unanswerable. See [pane](pane.md) and [view](view.md).
