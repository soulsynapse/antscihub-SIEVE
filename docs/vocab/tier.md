A place a frame can be answered from: one of an ordered stack, cheapest first,
over one open recording. Tiers are ranked by what an answer costs and not by
what it is worth, because they are all answering the same question — this
position, in this form — and differ only in what they give up to answer it
sooner. The store's budget of decoded frames is the ground floor; the chunk
store, the display proxy and the source itself are the rest.

A tier is a place, never a policy. `serve.Route` names which tier produced a
frame, and the two lists are deliberately not one list: three routes come out
of the held tier — exactly the form asked, another form derived on the way
out, and a nearby instant at the right form — and two more name outcomes with
no tier behind them at all, the position that will never exist and the request
nothing owes an answer to. Which threshold let a nearby frame through is
policy inside the tier, not a different place the frame came from.

The word predates this file by the whole storage substrate: `store.py`,
`chunks.py`, `proxy.py` and `serve.py` were written one at a time and each
reached for it unprompted, which is why it is recorded rather than decided.
`Serving` in `serve.py` is the stack itself, and it is the only thing that
knows the order.
