Anything a [pane](pane.md) can house — the occupant, not the space. "Surface"
used to mean this and was dropped for doing double duty: a thing a pane houses,
and a thing paint lands on. A view names only the first, and the word it was
dropped in favour of is still being used for both — see
[surface](surface.md), where that is the open question.

## Where it lives

`gui/view/` is one folder per view; `gui/primitives/view.py` is the chassis
every one of them stands in, a titled head band over a content area.
